## Handoff status as of October 13

Diagnosis, Probe, Safe Binary, and Controller have frozen implementations and validation/test artifacts; the owners' handoff file is `docs/DIAGNOSIS_PROBE_CONTROLLER_HANDOFF.md` on the remote branch. E consumes their final `ControllerDecision` and does not retrain or adjust the above modules. There are normalized diagnosis annotations for 500 samples each of FEVER/NQ, as well as NQ expansion annotations; these belong to the project's fixed experiment samples and are not four-class failure annotations of the full original FEVER/NQ datasets.

E's `generation_repair` interface has completed CPU contract validation against the upstream real `ControllerDecision` class: among the four actions, a call is made only once when routed to E, and zero calls after Safe Binary changes back to correct. 54 code and protocol tests pass in total. Additionally, the frozen classifier, 27 Probe calls, and 1 E call were actually run on T4; the 100 development samples show no net gain, and the other two types of compensation have not yet been executed in this integration.

E's fixed development cohort uses the training split intersection, excluding Diagnosis validation/test of both versions. v1–v3 show no accuracy gain so far; v4's cited candidate generation with the original answer hidden went 45→46 on 50 FEVER samples, but the new correct one cited the wrong entity; on 50 NQ samples it went 20→20, with 1 fixed and 1 broken, and F1 slightly decreased. The direct Reanswer comparison with the same extra budget had no gain. The 10 smoke samples are included in the 50 and cannot be counted twice. The engineering interface can be handed over; algorithmic effectiveness and the whole adaptive system are not yet complete.

`reranking` defaults to keep5 (reranking only), because the frozen500 retrieval-side comparison of keep3 lost 14 pieces of evidence containing the answer; that branch has not yet measured generation quality. Its 500-sample comparison includes 65 validation and 68 test IDs of Diagnosis v2; after selecting the default strategy, this batch of data should no longer serve as an unseen test set for the complete adaptive framework. This issue does not rewrite the frozen metrics of Diagnosis itself.

Next steps: freeze the v4 negative results → new utilisation-stratified cohort within training and semantic verification → validate E capability and frozen routing coverage separately → merge the three compensation operations → evaluate quality and full cost on shared data not used for strategy selection. A valid citation source does not mean the conclusion is supported; independent faithfulness/verifier still needs separate evaluation. E's final completion criteria are in [ANSWER_REPAIR.md](../../ANSWER_REPAIR.md).
