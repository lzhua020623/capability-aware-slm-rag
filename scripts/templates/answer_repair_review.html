Review notes and label criteria

Scope: among the frozen C1 FEVER/NQ 500 each, all utilisation candidates in the Diagnosis v1/v2 training intersection (27) plus 10 correct controls. Both validation/test versions are excluded; 32 are newly included this round, 5 from the previous development round. This filter does not represent overall class proportions.

utilisation: the original answer has a substantive error, and original Top-5 already has sufficient, clear support. accessibility: the needed information is in Top-5, but conflict, noise, or ranking makes it hard to use. These two causes cannot always be decided from one output; keep boundary doubts. insufficiency: Top-5 lacks information needed to answer. correct: the original answer is semantically correct for the question even if it does not match gold word-for-word.

Time-sensitive questions are reviewed at the frozen-corpus date. Do not fill Top-5 from current knowledge; flag suspected gold errors separately. Human choices all start blank. Only clicking “Confirm this human review” counts as reviewed; edits after entry return it to draft. If checking is unfinished, defer and write the doubt.
